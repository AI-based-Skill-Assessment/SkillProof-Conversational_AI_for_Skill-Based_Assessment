## What is this study about?
SkillProof is a web application that conducts a short, automated technical interview and checks the candidate's identity with face and voice matching. We are measuring how accurately it recognises people and how closely its scores agree with human interviewers.

## What will I do?
- Create an account on a laptop provided by the researcher (about 3 minutes).
- Register your face (camera) and your voice (reading one sentence).
- Take one spoken interview of 5–7 questions about a skill you have (about 10 minutes).
- Optionally, take part in an "impostor" test, where you answer questions during another participant's session so the system's ability to detect a different person can be measured.
- Optionally, tell us a demographic group (for example gender) so we can check whether the system works equally well for different groups. You can skip this.

Total time: about 15 minutes.

## What data is collected?
- **Face and voice templates:** numeric vectors computed from your camera and microphone. No photos, videos or audio recordings are stored.
- **Live samples:** the same kind of numeric vectors, taken during the interview, used to measure accuracy.
- **Your spoken answers:** sent to a speech-to-text service (Groq / Whisper) to be transcribed. The audio is not stored; the text transcript is.
- **Scores and proctoring events** produced by the system.

## How will my data be used and protected?
- Transcripts shown to human raters are anonymised (your name is removed).
- Results are reported only in aggregate (for example, error rates across all participants). You will not be identifiable in any publication.
- Data is stored on the researcher's computer and used only for this study.
- Your face and voice templates and live samples are deleted at the latest **180 days** after the study, and immediately if you ask. You can also delete them yourself at any time in the app (Settings → Delete biometric data).

## Your rights
- Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time without giving a reason and without any consequence.
- You may ask for your data to be deleted at any time by contacting the researcher.
- Your score has no effect on any course, grade or placement.
